# Proposal

## Why

Milestones (first 100 stars, years on GitHub, many languages) motivate and tell a story. Trophy and achievement cards are among the most used widgets, but none follow a profile's theme.

## What Changes

- New optional achievements card with original badge artwork per theme.
- Each achievement has levels with documented thresholds and a progress hint to the next level.
- Only earned achievements are shown by default.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `achievements-card`: Optional card with earned achievements, levels and thresholds.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Impact

New card module and assets, data from existing queries plus account age and organizations.

## Out of Scope

- Achievements based on private data.
- GitHub's own achievement badges (not available in the API).

## Prior Art

ryo-ma/github-profile-trophy (MIT) and metrics' achievements plugin (MIT). Idea only; artwork and thresholds are original.
